To improve the accuracy and reliability of results, the follow future work are recommended:

1. Improved Measurement Techiniques and Re-evaluation of material.

Future testing should incorporate additional measuring devices such as Extensometers, Digital Image Correlation (DIC) or high speed cameras. These devices would isolate specimen deformation and eliminate machine compliance effects. Incorporating strain gauges would also allow direct measurement of material strain, independent validation of deflection of the rim barrels and account for the shaft geometry. These additional measures would result in more accurate material data to bridge the gap between simulations and experiments.

2. Wheel Centre Redesign.

Factoring out stress concentrations, the stress is 1075.3 MPa. These stress concentrations could be affected by the Keizer wheel centres. The current part has limited contact area and a redesign could improve load distribution and reduce stresses.

3. Modified jig for compression test with tires.

The steel plate for the shaft was modified to be able to fit the new jig. This modification did not take into account the tapered shaft. As a result, the plate does not fit perfectly onto the shaft as intended and requires careful preloading to ensure the assembly is tight. For testing of the rims, strain gauges should be used to measure the deflection of the material. In addition to isolating the displacement of the applicator, it will also be able to collect data on both barrels of the rim and allow for finer control over simulating the layups of each barrel to maximise the layup and weight.
